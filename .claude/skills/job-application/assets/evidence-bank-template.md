## Proof stories

> Five to eight of these. Each one should survive fifteen minutes of interview questioning.
> Duplicate the block per story.

### Story: [short name]

- **Context:** what the situation was, what was broken or missing
- **My specific action:** what *you* did, not what the team did
- **Outcome:** the number, or the concrete thing that exists now that didn't before
- **What it proves:** which capability this is evidence for
- **The follow-up question I'd get:** and the honest answer to it
